QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How does mobile massage work?

You book an appointment, provide your location and we bring the required equipment to your home, hotel or office.

Do I need to know which massage to book?

No. If you're unsure, complete the Wellness Check or discuss your needs during your consultation.

Can I choose whether we talk?

Absolutely. You can choose quiet time, music, conversation, jokes, a listening ear or a combination depending on your preferences.

What should I do if I have a medical condition?

Tell us during your consultation. Some situations may require modifications or advice from an appropriate healthcare professional before massage.

Can I request a specific therapist?

Therapist requests can be discussed when booking, subject to availability.

Do you offer corporate bookings?

Yes. Contact us for customised corporate wellness and event packages.
